# Viral Shorts Finder

## Objective
Find promising YouTube Shorts and gaming content opportunities.

## Research process

1. Ask the user for a niche, such as GTA gaming.
2. Search YouTube for relevant videos.
3. Collect publicly available video statistics.
4. Calculate engagement rate:
   (Likes + Comments) / Views * 100
5. Calculate views per subscriber:
   Views / Channel Subscribers
6. Calculate average views per day:
   Views / Video Age in Days
7. Identify videos performing unusually well compared with their channels.
8. Rank promising videos.
9. Analyse titles, topics and opening hooks.
10. Generate original video ideas based on patterns.

## Output

Create a report containing:

- Video title
- Video URL
- Channel name
- Subscriber count
- Views
- Likes
- Comments
- Engagement rate
- Views per subscriber
- Views per day
- Why the video may be performing well
- Original content ideas

## Rules

- Use authorized YouTube data sources.
- Never invent statistics.
- Do not assume high views guarantee future success.
- Do not recommend copying another creator's content.
- Clearly identify missing data.
